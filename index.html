        About TeachBack AI

        TeachBack AI is built on the Feynman Technique — the study method used by top students worldwide: if you can explain a topic in simple words, you truly understand it; where your explanation breaks, your understanding has gaps.

        Our AI listens to your explanation like a teacher, scores it, and shows you exactly what to fix. It was created by a microbiology student, for students everywhere.
